How to use the pack

Print the whole thing once and keep it in a folder, or print a single sheet at the end of a session and hand it over warm. Most people do the second. A sheet given at the moment someone has just felt a pattern work is worth more than a pack given at the start of an engagement, because it carries the memory of the thing rather than the idea of it.

Your browser's print dialogue decides the paper size. The sheets are laid out for A4 and for US Letter, one technique per page, with the margins set so nothing is cut off by a home printer. If you would rather have a PDF, choose your browser's "Save as PDF" destination in the same dialogue; there is no separate download to keep track of and no file that goes out of date the next time a caution is reworded.

What is on each sheet

Every sheet has the same five parts, so a client who collects two or three of them learns where to look.

  • The pattern, written as counts — for example Inhale 4 · Hold 7 · Exhale 8 — with the length of one full round underneath it.
  • The steps, numbered, in the order you would say them out loud.
  • When people use it, in plain language, with no claim that it treats anything.
  • What to be careful about, taken from the same contraindication list the timers use. It is not softened for the handout.
  • The address of the timer, typed out as text so it can be read and re-typed by a person. There is no QR code on these sheets, deliberately: a lot of the people who receive them are in a waiting room, a classroom or a hospital ward where a phone camera is awkward, and a short address they can copy is kinder than a square they cannot scan.

The sheets are generated from the same data file that drives every timer on the site. When a caution is improved, the handout changes with it, and there is no stale PDF sitting in someone's downloads folder contradicting the live page.

What to be careful about

Two patterns carry more risk than the rest. The 4-7-8 pattern includes a seven-second hold, and the energizing pattern involves fast breathing followed by a hold. Both can feel unpleasant or unsafe for some people, and the sheets say so. Please use your own judgement about who receives which sheet, and read the cautions before you hand one over rather than after.

Nothing on these sheets is medical care and none of it replaces treatment. They are prompts for a practice, written for someone who already has a practitioner in front of them. If you are giving one to a person who does not, the medical disclaimer is the thing to read first.

What happens to the details you type

The five practitioner fields are kept in your browser's own storage under hmb.practitioner. They are not sent to us, they are not sent to anyone else, and they never leave the device you typed them on. Clearing your browser data clears them. A logo URL is loaded directly from your own site by your own browser when the sheet is drawn; the image is never uploaded here and only https addresses are accepted.

The client links you build carry your name, your message and — if you tick the branding box — your licence token, all encoded inside the link itself. That is the entire mechanism. There is no record on our side of which link you made, who you sent it to, or whether they opened it. The privacy attestation states this formally and is dated and signed, and the Data Processing Addendum is there if a practice manager or a school needs something to file.
