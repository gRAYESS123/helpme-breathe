Why one plan

Most people want the same three things from a breathing timer once it has become a habit: to keep the habit, to make the timer feel like their own, and to be left alone while they breathe. Practitioners want those and a licence they can show to whoever asks. Splitting that across tiers means explaining the split instead of the product, so there is one plan and everything is in it.

Someone arriving here at three in the morning because they cannot sleep, or in the middle of a panic attack, still gets a working timer and nothing else. The plan is for the people who come back.

Common questions

What is free?

Every technique, every session length, the audio and vibration cues and the offline install are free to use today, without an account. The plan adds the tools around the practice listed on this page. It never takes the timer away.

How does the free trial work?

The trial lasts three days from the moment you start it. A payment card is required to begin and is not charged during the trial. When the three days end, the card is charged the price shown at checkout, $10 for the monthly plan or $100 for the yearly plan in US dollars plus any tax due in your country, and the subscription begins, unless you cancel first. Cancelling during the trial takes two clicks from your account page. One free trial per person.

Do I need an account?

For the plan, yes. You sign in with a link sent to your email or with a Google account; there is no password to remember. The account holds your plan so it follows you between your phone, tablet and laptop. Your practice history stays in your browser unless you export it.

Can I cancel?

Any time, from your account page, in two clicks. Cancelling stops the next charge and you keep access until the end of the period you have already paid for. There is no cancellation fee and no one will email you to talk you out of it.

Is there a refund?

Fourteen days, unconditional. Email contact@helpmebreath.com within fourteen days of a charge and the money goes back to the card you paid with. No reason required, and no follow-up questions.

Monthly or yearly?

They include exactly the same things. Yearly costs $100 against $120 for twelve monthly payments, so it is two months free for anyone who already knows they will keep the habit. If you start monthly you can switch to yearly from your account at any time, and the months you have already paid are credited.

I teach breathing to clients. Is that included?

Yes. The commercial-use licence, the white-label embed for your own site, co-branded client session links, the printable handout pack with your name on it, class and projector mode, and the signable data-processing agreement are all part of the one plan. There is no separate practitioner tier.

What if your payment provider disappears?

Payments are handled by a merchant of record, and providers do sometimes close accounts or exit markets. Your plan is honoured for the period you have paid for whatever happens to the provider, and if one has to be replaced you are moved to the replacement at no cost. Email contact@helpmebreath.com with the address you signed in with.

A note on what this is

This is a timer and a set of tools for a breathing practice. Nothing here is medical care and none of it replaces treatment, a clinician or a crisis line. Some breathing patterns are not suitable for everyone; breath holds and fast breathing in particular carry real contraindications, which are written on each technique's own page. Read the medical disclaimer before you start, and the privacy policy if you want to know exactly what is stored.
