Why one plan

Most people want the same three things from a breathing timer once it has become a habit: to keep the habit, to make the timer feel like their own, and to be left alone while they breathe. Splitting those across tiers means explaining the split instead of the product, so there is one plan and everything is in it.

Someone arriving here at three in the morning because they cannot sleep, or in the middle of a panic attack, still gets a working timer and nothing else. The plan is for the people who come back.

Any time, from your account page, in two clicks. Cancelling stops the next charge and you keep access until the end of the period you have already paid for. There is no cancellation fee and no one will email you to talk you out of it.

A note on what this is

This is a timer and a set of tools for a breathing practice. Nothing here is medical care and none of it replaces treatment, a clinician or a crisis line. Some breathing patterns are not suitable for everyone; breath holds and fast breathing in particular carry real contraindications, which are written on each technique's own page. Read the medical disclaimer before you start, and the privacy policy if you want to know exactly what is stored.
