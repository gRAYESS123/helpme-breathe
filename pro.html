Buy it once. Breathe forever.

Pro adds the tools around your practice for one payment. The practice itself stays free.

Georges Rayess — writes and maintains Help Me Breathe. Not a clinician.

Published 2026-09-09 · Updated 2026-09-10

Help Me Breathe is a free breathing timer, and it stays one. Pro is a single payment that unlocks the things you only want once breathing has become a habit: your own patterns, saved and shareable; your practice history; sound to breathe to; a screen that will not blind you at two in the morning. There is no subscription, no account and no password.

Founding price: $14 for the first 100 people, then $19.

What stays free, in plain words

All seven techniques — 4-7-8, box breathing, heart coherence, cyclic sighing, extended exhale, triangle breathing and the energizing breath — stay free and unlimited forever. So do every session length, the audio and vibration cues, the offline install, your saved settings and your session history. Nothing that works today will ever move behind a payment. If we add a new technique, it lands on the free tier too. That is not a promotion; it is the shape of the thing.

The reason is simple. Someone arriving at this site at three in the morning because they cannot sleep, or in the middle of a panic attack, should get a working timer and nothing else. Putting a price in front of that would be indefensible, and it would also be bad business: the free timer is what brings people here. Pro is for the people who come back.

What Pro actually adds

Your own patterns, saved

Four sliders: inhale, hold, exhale, hold. Anyone can build a pattern and run it — that part is free, and it is the fastest way to find the rhythm that suits you rather than the one a book recommended. Pro saves patterns under names you choose, so "wind-down" is one tap away, and turns any pattern into a link. Send that link to someone and their timer opens already set to it, whether or not they have paid for anything.

A record of what you have actually done

Every completed session is already stored in your browser. Pro turns that into a current streak, a longest streak, total minutes, a twelve-week heatmap and a breakdown by technique, plus a CSV export if you want the numbers somewhere else. None of it leaves your device — there is no server holding your history, which is also why we cannot restore it if you clear your browser.

Something to breathe to

Six ambient soundscapes, no voices and no music with a beat, mixed to sit under the pacing tones rather than compete with them. They cache for offline use after the first play, so a flight or a basement does not silence them.

Night mode

The site already follows your device: set the phone to dark and the page comes up dark, paid or not. Pro adds the switch, so you can hold the page dark on a bright device or light on a dark one, and it asks the browser to keep the screen awake while a session runs, so the phone does not lock halfway through.

No ads, anywhere

The guides and comparison articles carry a small number of ads to pay hosting costs. Pro removes them site-wide. There has never been an ad on the timer screen itself, on the anxiety page or on the panic-attack page, for anyone, and there never will be.

The Protocol Pack

Print-ready cards for four situations — falling asleep, interrupting a panic spiral, a focus sprint and the few minutes before something you are dreading — with a practice log you can fill in by hand. Included with Pro at no extra cost.

Working with clients?

If you teach breathing to other people — as a therapist, a yoga or fitness teacher, a coach or a school counsellor — the Practitioner licence is the one to read about. It covers commercial use, removes our attribution from an embed you put on your own site, lets you set your logo and colours, and gives you a link you can send a client with the technique and length already chosen. It also comes with a signed data-processing agreement and a plain attestation that no client data leaves the browser, which is usually the part your insurer or your employer asks about.

Already bought Pro? Paste the licence key from your receipt email to unlock this browser. Keys work on any device you use, up to the activation count for your plan. Common questions

Do I lose any of the free techniques if I do not buy Pro?

No. All seven techniques, every session length including unlimited, the audio and vibration cues, the offline install and your saved settings and history stay free and unlimited. Pro adds tools around the practice. It never takes the practice away.

What if your payment provider disappears?

Payments are handled by a merchant of record, and providers do sometimes close accounts or exit markets. Two things protect you. Your licence lives in your browser as a signed token, so a lifetime unlock keeps working through the offline grace path even when nothing can be reached. And if the provider goes away, keys are reissued through the replacement provider at no cost to you — email contact@helpmebreath.com with the address you paid from.

Do I need an account or a password?

No. There is no sign-up, no password and no database of users. Buying Pro emails you a licence key; pasting that key into this page unlocks the browser you pasted it into. Your practice history stays in your browser and is never uploaded.

How many devices does one Pro licence cover?

Six activations for Pro, which covers a phone, a tablet and a laptop with room to replace one. Practitioner covers twenty-five. If you run out because you changed devices, email contact@helpmebreath.com and the count is reset.

Is there a refund?

Fourteen days, unconditional. Email contact@helpmebreath.com within fourteen days of paying and you get the money back. No reason required, and no follow-up questions.

Should I buy the monthly plan or the lifetime unlock?

The lifetime unlock, in almost every case. At $3.99 a month the subscription costs more than the one-time price within five months. The monthly plan exists for people who want to try Pro without committing, and it can be cancelled at any time.

A note on what this is

This is a timer and a set of tools for a breathing practice. Nothing here is medical care and none of it replaces treatment, a clinician or a crisis line. Some breathing patterns are not suitable for everyone — breath holds and fast breathing in particular carry real contraindications, which are written on each technique's own page. Read the medical disclaimer before you start, and the privacy policy if you want to know exactly what is stored (almost nothing, all of it in your own browser).
