Who this is for

This licence is built for one practising person, working directly with the people in front of them. In practice that means:

  • Therapists and counsellors who want a between-session practice a client will actually do.
  • Yoga, breathwork and meditation teachers who run classes and want the pattern on the screen instead of counting for forty minutes.
  • Coaches, sports coaches and performance coaches building a pre-event routine.
  • School staff — counsellors, SENCOs, form tutors — who need something calm on the projector that will not show an advert halfway through.
  • Independent facilitators running small workplace sessions, where the client expects your branding and not a stranger's.

Who this is not for

This is not an enterprise product and it is not sold through procurement. Help Me Breathe is one person: Georges Rayess, working as an individual, not a company. The three compliance documents below are the whole of what exists. They are honest, they are signable, and they are deliberately modest.

A worked example: from the session to the homework

Here is the whole flow, with a client who freezes at night and wants something to do about it. Nothing in it involves creating an account for anyone.

  1. In the room. You open the timer on your practice site — your logo in the corner, your accent colour on the circle — and set extended-exhale breathing for three minutes. You breathe with her once so she has felt it rather than only heard about it.
  2. You print the sheet. On the handout page you press print on the extended-exhale sheet. It comes out with your name, your practice and your phone number at the top, the pattern in large type, five steps, a short note on when it helps, and the cautions written out. She folds it into her bag.
  3. You build the homework link. Still on the handout page, you pick the technique, set three minutes, type one line — "Twice a day this week. Before bed is the one that matters." — and press copy.
  4. You send it. The link goes in your follow-up email. It opens a page with your name and your message at the top and the timer already set to three minutes of extended exhale. No download, no sign-up, no account and no advert — the site's cookie notice is the only thing she has to answer, and only once.
  5. Next session. She tells you she did it four nights out of seven. You know that because she told you, not because a dashboard told you. There is no dashboard. That is the design, not a missing feature.

The whole configuration for that link lives inside the link itself. Nothing about the client is written to a server, because there is no server that would hold it. If she forwards the link to her sister, her sister gets the same three minutes with your name on it, which is usually fine and occasionally a small marketing gift.

What this is not

Being plain about the limits is more useful to you than another list of benefits.

Common questions

Can I remove the Help Me Breathe attribution?

Yes, on the domains covered by your licence. The white-label embed renders with your logo and your accent colour and carries no Help Me Breathe branding at all while the licence is active. The free embed keeps its attribution link, and that link is always rel="nofollow noopener". There is no paid option to make it a followed link, on any tier, because widget-distributed links are named in Google's spam policy and selling one would put both of us at risk.

Can I use it in classes and sessions that I charge for?

Yes. That is exactly what the commercial-use grant covers: paid one-to-one sessions, paid group classes, workshops, corporate sessions you are paid to run, and the handouts you give the people who attend. You may not resell the licence itself, sublicense it to other practitioners, or present the tool as your own product.

What happens if I stop paying?

Nothing breaks and nothing goes dark. Your licence token expires within seven days of the subscription ending, after which the white-label embed reverts to the free attributed widget and the practitioner name block on the handout page switches back off. Every timer, every technique and every duration stays free and unlimited, for you and for your clients. Handouts you have already printed are yours, and links you have already sent keep working as plain unbranded session links.

What is the refund policy?

Fourteen days, unconditional. Ask within fourteen days of the charge and you get the full amount back, with no questions about why. Our merchant of record processes the refund to the card you paid with. After fourteen days the annual licence runs to the end of its term and simply does not renew if you cancel.

Do you store anything about my clients?

No. The timer runs entirely in the browser. There is no account, no client record, no session database and no server that receives what your client practised. Session settings and history stay in that person's own browser storage and are never transmitted to us. The only personal data we ever hold about the licence is the email address you give the merchant of record at checkout.

Will you complete our security questionnaire or sign a HIPAA BAA?

No, and it is fairer to say so before you buy. Help Me Breathe is one person, not a company with a compliance department. We publish a signable Data Processing Addendum, a privacy attestation and a two-page security overview, and those three documents are the whole of what we can offer. If your organisation requires SOC 2, a HIPAA Business Associate Agreement, single sign-on or a vendor questionnaire, please do not buy this licence.

How many devices does one licence cover?

Twenty-five activations on the Practitioner licence. An activation is one browser on one device, so your laptop, your phone, the studio tablet and the room computer each use one. Studio raises the domain limit rather than the activation count: ten domains instead of one.
