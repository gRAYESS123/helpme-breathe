Breathing techniques

4-7-8: in for four, hold seven, out for eight
Box breathing: four equal counts in, hold, out, hold
Coherence: five seconds in, five seconds out
Cyclic sighing: a double inhale then a long exhale
Extended exhale: four seconds in, six seconds out
Triangle: three counts in, hold, out
Energizing breath: fast rhythmic breathing, read the safety note first

Before you start, and common questions

Who should be careful

Patterns that hold the breath or speed it up are not right for everyone. Ask a clinician first if you are pregnant, or if you live with a heart condition, uncontrolled high blood pressure, epilepsy or another seizure disorder, asthma, COPD or another respiratory condition, glaucoma, or if you have had surgery recently. If you live with panic attacks, breath-holds can reproduce sensations you would rather not invite; the patterns without holds — extended exhale, cyclic sighing and heart coherence — are the safer starting point. Practise sitting or lying down, never while driving, cycling or in water. Stop if you feel dizzy, tingly, short of breath or simply unwell. This site is not medical care and does not replace it; the full wording is in the medical disclaimer.

What is the 4-7-8 breathing pattern?

Breathe in through your nose for four seconds, hold for seven, then breathe out through your mouth for eight. The timer counts it for you and can play a soft tone at each change. The seven-second hold is the part to shorten or skip if it feels like a struggle.

How does box breathing work?

Four equal counts: breathe in for four seconds, hold for four, breathe out for four, then stay empty for four. It repeats for as long as your session lasts.

Do I need an account, and does it cost anything?

No account, and all seven patterns are free with no time limit. Your settings and session history stay in your own browser and are never uploaded.

Is this safe for everyone?

No. Read the caution above before you choose a pattern with a hold or with fast breathing. If you are unsure, start with extended exhale or cyclic sighing, neither of which holds the breath.
