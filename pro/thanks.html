What happens next

Your receipt

The receipt arrives by email from Stripe, the merchant of record named as the seller on your card statement. If it is not in your inbox within a few minutes, check the spam folder before writing to us.

Your other devices

Sign in on any other phone, tablet or laptop with an email link or a Google account, and the plan is there. There is no key to paste and no password to remember; the plan follows the account, not the browser.

The new tools

Open any timer and look under the circle: "Custom pattern", "Your practice" and "Night mode" are there, plus the soundscapes once they are enabled. Nothing else about the timer changes.

The first three days are free. Your card is not charged during the trial, and on day three it is charged the price shown at checkout automatically unless you cancel first. One free trial per person. Cancel any time from your account page, in two clicks, and access continues to the end of the period you have paid for. A fourteen-day unconditional refund applies to any charge, as set out in the refund policy. Email contact@helpmebreath.com if anything at all is wrong, including if you simply changed your mind.
